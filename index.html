Entry rules, checked on every trade

  1. Trend foundMark the trend on the chart before anything else.
  2. BOSWait for a break of structure in the trend direction.
  3. Zone tappedWait for price to pull back and tap your supply or demand zone.
  4. Entry takenTake the entry with one of the three entry types below.
  5. Zone validConfirm the zone you traded from was a valid zone.

Entry types

Aggressive
Enter as price taps the zone.
Conservative
Wait for confirmation after the tap, then enter.
BOC
Enter on the break of the candle (BOC).

New York time

Nairobi times follow New York daylight saving automatically. Change a time and it saves.

Exit rules

  • Stop: beyond the far edge of the zone, wicks included. Skip the trade if it needs more than 60 pts.
  • Target: 2R by default (about 45 to 50 pts). 3R (about 85 to 120 pts) when you trade with the daily bias.
  • Time stop: close any open trade by 11:30 ET.
  • No setup by 11:15 ET: the day is done. A no-trade day is a valid result.
  • Every trade records a planned exit and the actual exit, so early exits show up in Analytics as points left on the table.
