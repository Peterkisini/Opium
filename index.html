Top-down routine

  1. Weekend · Weekly chartMark the weekly trend, last week's high and low, and the supply and demand zones still in play. Log the weekly bias.
  2. Each morning · Daily, 4H, 1HConfirm or change the daily bias. Note which zone price is heading toward.
  3. Before NY open · 5mMark the trend, wait for BOS, then wait for price to return to the zone.
  4. End of day · ReviewMark each bias call correct or wrong so the accuracy below stays honest.

Entry rules, checked on every trade

  1. Trend foundMark the trend on the chart before anything else.
  2. BOSWait for a break of structure in the trend direction.
  3. Zone tappedWait for price to pull back and tap your supply or demand zone.
  4. Entry takenTake the entry with one of the three entry types below.
  5. Zone validConfirm the zone you traded from was a valid zone.
